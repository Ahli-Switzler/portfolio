Rumple in the Jungle

Love’s Alibi, September 2026. Built around a Rumple Minze draft launch.

A single elimination bartender tournament with UFC-style walkouts, a live mystery ingredient wheel, and crowd hype scored as part of every bout.

100+ RSVPs, 11 bartenders, 10 bouts, and a room at capacity for three straight hours with no drop-off.

My role: co-created the concept, designed the bracket and rules, wrote the run of show, coordinated competitors and venue, ran the night’s music and timing, and wrote the recap. See the post

The Great Summer Daiq Off

Lost & Found, July 2026. Presented with Trovador Rum.

A citywide daiquiri competition between Nashville bars.

175 guests, 14 bars competing, and 22,700 social reach on a very small budget.

My role: created the concept, recruited all 14 bars and the judges, and served as point of contact for every bartender, including material pickup. Secured sponsored juice from Verde, represented Trovador as its brand ambassador, and handled all graphic design and social outreach. On the night, I ran the event on site and MC’d, then wrote the social recap. See the post

Potions & Paws

Lost & Found, October 2026. Benefiting Pawster.

A Halloween cocktail night for a Nashville dog fostering nonprofit, with a costume contest where the dogs go first. Spirits brands build featured cocktails, and $2 from each one goes to Pawster, rung through the register as its own line so the total is verifiable. Year two moved to a bigger venue and a bigger build after a smaller first year at Pearl Diver in 2025.

My role: concept, sponsor pitch, run of show, floor plan, print and digital assets, and MC. See the year two recap

Hot Takes Night

A live opinion showdown where people defend their worst takes in front of a crowd and a panel of judges. Sponsored by Bacardi, then Montucky Cold Snacks as Hot Takes & Cold Snacks, with a themed cocktail menu I designed.

My role: format, sponsor partnerships, menu and promo design, and MC.
